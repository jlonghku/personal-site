01 — Research

Connecting physical insight with data intelligence.

My work develops trustworthy ways to observe, simulate, and predict water systems across scales — from constructed wetlands to cities and entire watersheds.

R / 01∆

Intelligent Ecohydrological Modelling

Integrating process-based models with deep learning, knowledge distillation, and cross-resolution learning for accurate, interpretable predictions.

R / 02≈

Urban Flooding

Hydrological connectivity, drainage-system behaviour, and data-informed modelling for more resilient urban environments.

R / 03◌

Water Quality & Wetlands

Hydrodynamic, biochemical, and ecological responses to changing hydrology, informed by long-term field observations.

R / 04⌁

Environmental Decision Support

Coupled modelling frameworks that translate climate, land-use, and population scenarios into evidence for ecosystem management.

Selected work

Research across systems & scales.

01

Watershed scale · Modelling + AI

Puget Sound Integrated Modelling Framework

Coupled atmosphere–land cover–marine modelling to examine streamflow, nitrogen dynamics, forest–hydrology interactions, and ecosystem outcomes across the Puget Sound basin.

02

Urban scale · Connectivity + Optimisation

Resilient Urban Drainage & Flood Modelling

Combining SWMM, graph theory, and optimisation to evaluate hydrological connectivity and improve drainage performance across urban green infrastructure systems.

03

Ecosystem scale · Field + Process models

Constructed Wetlands & Aquatic Systems

Long-term monitoring and coupled SWMM, OpenFOAM, and EFDC simulations to understand hydraulic, water-quality, and ecological responses under changing conditions.
