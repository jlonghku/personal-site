# Long Jiang — Academic Website

Personal academic website for Dr Long Jiang, Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.

The site is available in English and Simplified Chinese, with a language switch in the header.

## Highlights

- Research themes in ecohydrological modelling, urban flooding, water quality, and environmental AI
- Selected projects, publications, experience, teaching, service, and honours
- Links to PolyU, Google Scholar, ORCID, and GitHub

## Structure

- `public/` — static site files
- `public/index.html` — English homepage
- `public/zh/index.html` — Chinese homepage
- `vercel.json` — deployment configuration
